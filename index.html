FP-10 MIDI Tester

Connects this phone to the Roland FP-10 over Bluetooth or USB to play notes, try every tone, change settings and record.

1 · Connect

Not connected

Bluetooth needs Chrome (Brave and Firefox block it). Turn the piano on, tap Bluetooth and pick FP-10 from the list. No other app is needed. If the piano isn't listed, close the Roland Piano App or any MIDI BLE app, because the piano accepts only one Bluetooth connection at a time. If it still isn't listed, Forget FP-10 in Android's Bluetooth settings and try again.

3 · Record

Records the keys, how hard you play them and the pedal.

This records MIDI, not audio: Play makes the piano play your take back exactly. Save .mid to open it in any music app or notation editor. The screen stays on while recording.

5 · Play notes
