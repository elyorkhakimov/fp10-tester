FP-10 MIDI Tester

Checks that this phone can play notes on the Roland FP-10 and change its settings.

1 · Connect

  1. USB: phone USB-C → FP-10 "Computer" port (USB-B). Or Bluetooth: connect the piano with a MIDI BLE app first.
  2. Tap Connect and allow MIDI access when Chrome asks.
Not connected

2 · Play notes

Dynamics plays the same chord six times, each louder. You should hear clear steps.

3 · Piano settings (Roland SysEx)

If the piano answers "Read volume back", two-way SysEx works and an app can both set and check settings.

4 · MIDI log

Play keys on the piano: they should show up here as incoming notes.
